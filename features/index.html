The ticket's true cost.

A traffic ticket is never just the fine. The moment Ticket Fighter reads your citation, it breaks down what the ticket will really cost you — court costs, the New Jersey surcharge, the points added to your license, and the three-year insurance increase those points trigger.

Most tickets don't even print a dollar amount, so if the fine isn't detected you just tap to add it and the total updates instantly. Suddenly that "$500 moving violation" is $1,433–$2,333 out of your pocket — and that's exactly why it's worth fighting.

4-Digit PIN Lock

Vault and Doc Wallet are locked behind a 4-digit PIN. If your phone is taken or grabbed, the officer cannot browse your recordings or documents without your authorization. Hardware-backed encryption on the device.

GPS Location Stamp

When you allow location access, every audio recording is stamped with the GPS coordinates of where it started and where it stopped, embedded in the local metadata file. Documents WHERE the encounter happened for your own records. Stays on your device. Never transmitted.

Smart Notifications

Reminders for document expirations (license, registration, insurance) and upcoming court dates. Everything is scheduled on your device locally — no server connection required, no data sent to anyone, no third-party reminder service.
