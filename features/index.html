FEATURES

Everything CopStopReady does for you. On your phone. No servers. No accounts. No analytics.

CopStopReady was built to protect you two ways — and both can save you money. On the road, it puts you in control during a stop: you know your rights, you stay calm, and a routine stop stays routine instead of spiraling into charges you didn't deserve. Off the road, it guards your wallet — watching every renewal date and every car fine you owe, so a date you forgot never turns into a $50, $500, or $1,000 mistake.

🎙️
Audio Recording

Tap the mic and audio records straight to your PIN-locked, on-device Vault — never uploaded. One tap and you're recording; the same screen shows the lines to say to the officer.

🗂️
Vault

Every recording is stored locally on your device and PIN-locked, tagged with the date and time so you can find the one you need without scrolling through files called "audio_001.m4a". Browse, play back, and share with your lawyer.

The instant a recording saves, CopStopReady stamps it with a unique fingerprint tied to that exact file. Tap Verify any time — even months later — and it checks the recording against that fingerprint; a match is proof it hasn’t changed since the second you made it. Hand a lawyer or court a proof sheet they can confirm on their own. It’s tamper-evident — it can’t stop a file from being changed, but it will always show if one was.

📇 Doc Wallet

Doc Wallet keeps your driver's license, registration, insurance card, and inspection sticker on your phone — no uploads, no cloud. It all stays on this phone, period.

Your wallet is locked behind your PIN — the same PIN that protects your Vault. Your PIN is encrypted in your phone's hardware-backed secure storage.

How your reminders turn on

For your license, registration, and insurance, just take the picture. CSR reads the expiration date right off the doc and switches your reminders on automatically — there's nothing to type.

Your inspection sticker is the only exception. A sticker's date is too small to scan reliably, so you enter it yourself, once. The moment you save it, those reminders switch on too.

What you get

  • Store all four — license, registration, insurance, and inspection sticker
  • Color-coded expiry tracking (green / yellow / red)
  • Renewal reminders on every doc at 90, 60, 30, 14, 7, and 3 days before each expires
  • Renew Now — open a saved doc and jump straight to the official renewal page (New Jersey license & registration today, more states coming; insurance opens a search for your carrier).

Once a doc is saved, just open Doc Wallet — each one shows a color-coded countdown of the days left before it expires.

Hear the alert

This is the exact siren your phone sounds when a renewal is coming due — tap it so you'll always know it when you hear it.

Turn your volume up. Plays your real in-app alert sound.

CopStopReady is built privacy-first — nothing you save ever leaves your phone. Doc Wallet puts your license, registration, insurance, and inspection right in your pocket, always ready.

More money in your wallet

Legal in New Jersey

🎟️ Fines Wallet

Fines Wallet keeps every car fine, ticket, and bill with a due date right here on your phone — a tow bill, an E-ZPass violation, a parking or traffic ticket, a red-light or speed-camera notice, a toll. Anything car-related you have to pay by a date.

100% private

Nothing leaves this phone. No servers, no analytics, no telemetry. No account, no sign-up, nothing uploaded — your fines live on this phone and nowhere else.

How it works

Snap a picture of the fine (optional), type the due date, and hit Save. Your reminders switch on automatically — there is nothing else to set up.

Your reminders

CopStopReady reminds you at 60, 30, 14, 7, 3, and 1 days before it's due, and again the day it's due. Some fines aren't dated a full 60 days out — and that's built in: your reminders adjust to each due date automatically, sending only the alerts that still fit the window. So you're always warned in time, no matter how short the notice. Mark a fine Paid and its reminders stop.

Color-coded countdown

Each fine shows the days left before it is due — green when you have time, yellow as it nears, red when it is almost due or past.

Keep the proof

The photo you snap is your proof you had the fine and when it was due — handy if a payment is ever disputed.

More money in your wallet

Miss a due date and a fine can snowball into late fees, penalties, even a suspended license. Fines Wallet keeps you one step ahead of every deadline.

⚖️ NJ Ticket Fines

Got a New Jersey ticket and want to know what it’ll really cost? The Fines screen breaks down speeding, moving violations, and document & serious offenses — each with an estimate and a true-cost calculator that adds it all up.

Estimates only

Every price shown is an estimate. Most payable tickets add about $33 in court costs, and points on your license can raise your insurance for years. Amounts vary by court and can change.

What the colors mean

  • Green — no points, so no insurance spike. A no-point downgrade can still carry a higher fine.
  • Yellow — two or three points, and your insurance starts to climb.
  • Red — four or more points, maybe a court appearance. The most expensive kind.

See a ticket’s true cost

Tap any fine to open its breakdown. Enter your yearly insurance rate and CopStopReady estimates the real damage — the fine, about $33 in court costs, and the insurance increase over three years. Court-level tickets show the details to expect instead.

Log a ticket you got

Get a ticket or fine with a due date? Tap Add a Fine, snap a picture, add the due date, and tap Save. Done.

Reminders switch on automatically — no setup

CopStopReady alerts you at 60, 30, 14, 7, 3, and 1 days before it’s due, and again the day it’s due — with a siren alert you can’t miss. So a $50 slip never snowballs into a $1,000 one.
