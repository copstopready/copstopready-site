FEATURES

Everything CopStopReady does for you. On your phone. No servers. No accounts. No analytics.

🎙️
Audio Recording

Tap the mic. Capture starts immediately. Survives lock screen, force-close, and low battery. Every recording is auto-titled with the GPS location and timestamp the moment it begins, so the court-grade evidence is ready before the encounter even ends.

🗂️
Vault

Every recording stored locally on your device. PIN-locked. Auto-named by location and time so you can find the one you need without scrolling through dozens of files called "audio_001.m4a". Example: "Stop on Raritan Avenue, May 21, 3:06pm". Browse, play back, share with your lawyer.

📇
Doc Wallet

Your license, registration, and insurance photos in one place. PIN-locked. Single-document mode lets you hand the phone to the officer showing ONLY what they asked for, without unlocking the rest of your phone or exposing other documents.

⚖️
Ticket FighterComing Soon

Coming in a future update. Take a photo of your ticket. We extract the statute, violation, fine, court date, and officer info, then generate a court-ready letter that finds the same procedural holes a traffic lawyer would charge $400 to identify. When it launches: $9.99 per scan, with a 60-day money-back guarantee if you are not satisfied with your court outcome. Limit one refund per customer.

📖
Rights Booklet

Every protection you have during a traffic stop, written in plain English. Short, clear, no legal jargon. Two taps from anywhere in the app. Know what you can refuse, what you must comply with, and where the constitutional line sits — before the officer is at your window.

🚨
CSR Coach

In-the-moment guidance during an active stop. Step-by-step reminders for what to say and not say, what to hand over and what to keep, when to invoke your rights and how. Designed to be glanceable while your hands are on the wheel.

🔒
4-Digit PIN Lock

Vault and Doc Wallet are locked behind a 4-digit PIN. If your phone is taken or grabbed, the officer cannot browse your recordings or documents without your authorization. Hardware-backed encryption on the device.

🛰️
GPS Location Stamp

Every audio recording is automatically stamped with the exact GPS coordinates of where it started AND where it stopped, embedded directly in the local metadata file. Documents WHERE the encounter happened for court evidence and complaint filings. Stays on your device. Never transmitted.

🔔
Smart Notifications

Reminders for document expirations (license, registration, insurance) and upcoming court dates. Everything is scheduled on your device locally — no server connection required, no data sent to anyone, no third-party reminder service.

🌌
No Servers. No Analytics. No Telemetry.

This is not a marketing line. It is an architecture decision. There is no cloud account. There is no backend. There is no analytics pipeline. CopStopReady cannot leak what does not exist on a server. Cannot be subpoenaed for data it does not have. Cannot sell what it never collected.

🌙
Moon Mode

Tactical, night-ops dark theme. Pure black background with glowing green grid lines designed to be readable in low light without blinding you at 2am on the shoulder of a dark road. Built for the actual conditions you will use this app in.

🇺🇸
Built for the Constitution

Rooted in the 4th and 5th Amendments. CopStopReady is not a legal service and does not give legal advice — it is a tool that helps you exercise rights you already have, made by people who believe you should not need a lawyer in the car to know what protections you carry as a U.S. citizen.

All of this. Free to use.

Right now, everything is free. Paid features are coming in a future update — Ticket Fighter ($9.99/scan) and Text Reminders ($1.99/mo). Until then, the whole app is free to use.
