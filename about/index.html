ABOUT

The whole product, in one paragraph.

CopStopReady™ was built for one reason: Americans should know what they can and cannot do during a traffic stop — and have the tools to protect themselves when the stop happens.

Every feature in this app — Rights Booklet, audio recording, Doc Wallet, Ticket Fighter — exists because a real person needed it in a real situation.

Why no servers? Servers create a target. Subpoenas, breaches, leaks — all impossible if the data isn’t there to take. CopStopReady doesn’t have an account system, doesn’t require an internet connection after install, and doesn’t see what you do inside the app. Not by choice we could reverse — by design we can’t.

Your data stays on your phone. Your rights stay with you. Your control stays in your hands.

That is the whole product.
