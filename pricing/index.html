What the scanner actually does

Ticket Fighter isn't an OCR app that reads your ticket back to you. It's a 17-layer intelligence engine, hand-built by the CSR team, that takes a single photo of your citation and runs it through the same procedural gauntlet a seasoned defense attorney would — in about thirty seconds, entirely on your phone.

The first layers read and reconstruct every field on the citation — statute code, violation, ticket number, defendant, officer badge, fine, plate, location, court, date and time. Each value is extracted, cross-checked, and confidence-scored, so a smudge, a glare, or a bad camera angle never slips a wrong number into your defense.

Then the dedicated analysis engines go hunting for the cracks. TimeEngine cross-references the violation timestamp, issue time, and court time for the inconsistencies lawyers use to challenge admissibility. CourtEngine validates the listed municipal court against the citation's jurisdiction and flags the mismatches that can void venue. ContradictionEngine checks every field against every other field — wrong vehicle, mismatched statute and violation, internally inconsistent fines — the officer errors that get cases dismissed when they're raised properly. EvidenceEngine maps exactly what the prosecution would have to prove, and what the citation fails to supply.

Finally, the FusionEngine weighs every layer at once and writes a court-ready letter calibrated to the single strongest defense your specific ticket supports — not a fill-in-the-blank template, but a real argument built around the exact holes the stack found. One photo goes in; the kind of defense a lawyer bills $400 to write comes out.
