What the scanner actually does

Ticket Fighter is not a simple OCR tool that reads text. It is a multi-engine analysis pipeline built from the ground up to find the same procedural cracks a good defense attorney looks for in a ticket. You take one photo. We do five passes simultaneously:

1. OCR extraction. Statute code, violation, ticket number, defendant info, officer badge, fine amount, location, plate, court date — all pulled off your physical citation.

2. Time analysis. The TimeEngine cross-checks the violation timestamp, the court time, and the issued time for inconsistencies that defense lawyers routinely use to challenge admissibility.

3. Court verification. The CourtEngine validates the listed municipal court against the citation jurisdiction and flags mismatches that void venue.

4. Contradiction detection. The ContradictionEngine cross-references every extracted field against every other field to catch the kind of officer errors — wrong vehicle, mismatched statute and violation, internally inconsistent fines — that get cases dismissed when raised properly.

5. Evidentiary gap analysis. The EvidenceEngine identifies what the prosecution would need to prove to convict, and what is missing from the citation that they cannot supply later.

The FusionEngine then weighs all five passes and generates a court-ready letter calibrated to the strongest defense your specific ticket supports — not a generic template, an actual argument built around the specific holes our pipeline found.
