FAQ

Real questions, straight answers. No fluff, no legalese hedging, no marketing-team approval needed.

01.Is it legal to record the police?

Yes. In all 50 states, you have a constitutional right to record law enforcement officers performing their duties in public places where you have a lawful right to be. Some states have wiretap laws that complicate audio-only recording of private conversations — but a traffic stop on a public road is not a private conversation. CSR records audio by default because audio survives a phone being pointed at the floor or sitting on the passenger seat. Always tell the officer you are recording. It is your right, but it is also a courtesy that de-escalates the encounter.

02.Does CSR work without internet?

Yes. After the initial install from Google Play, CSR does not need an internet connection to do its job. Recording, Vault playback, Doc Wallet, Ticket Fighter scans, the Rights Booklet, court letter generation — all of it runs entirely on your phone. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device, with no server and no network connection. The only thing that ever uses the network is the Play Store updating the app itself.

You can verify this yourself: install CSR, turn on airplane mode, and use the app normally. It just works.

03.Will the officer know I am recording?

Only if you tell them. CSR keeps recording in the background the moment you tap the mic — your screen can go dark and your phone can be in your pocket, and it keeps going.

We recommend telling the officer you are recording anyway. It is your legal right, it de-escalates the encounter, and secret recording can create legal complications in some contexts. Telling them is the strongest legal posture.

04.What happens to my recordings if I uninstall the app?

They are deleted with the app. CSR stores everything in the app’s private storage area, which Android wipes when you uninstall. This is part of the privacy architecture — nothing is left behind on the device, nothing was ever copied to a server.

If you need to keep a recording before uninstalling, share it out of the Vault to another app first (email it to yourself, send it to a cloud storage app of your choice, etc.).

05.Does the Ticket Fighter scanner actually work?

It runs five analysis engines in parallel on every ticket. TimeEngine checks for timestamp inconsistencies between violation, citation, and court appearance. CourtEngine validates the listed venue against the citation jurisdiction. ContradictionEngine cross-references every extracted field against every other field for officer errors. EvidenceEngine identifies what the prosecution would need to prove and what the citation fails to supply. FusionEngine weighs all four passes and writes the court-ready letter calibrated to the strongest defense your specific ticket supports.

A traffic lawyer reads your ticket top-to-bottom once, one set of eyes, one pass, and charges $400 to produce essentially the same document. Same finding power. Same legal weight. 1/40th the price.

This is not your average hobbyist scanner. Built by the CSR team. Tested on real tickets. Tuned for court.

If you are not satisfied with your court outcome, email us within 60 days of court date for a full refund. No questions asked. Limit one refund per customer.

06.What if my ticket is not from New Jersey?

v1.0 OCR is optimized for the New Jersey Uniform Traffic Ticket format. The scanner will attempt extraction on tickets from other states, but accuracy will be lower until we ship state-specific extractors. New York, Pennsylvania, and Connecticut are on the v1.1 roadmap with state-specific defense logic to follow.

If you got a ticket out of state and want to try the scanner anyway, the 60-day money-back guarantee still applies.

07.Why no iPhone version?

v1.0 ships Android-only because shipping one platform well beats shipping two platforms badly. The iOS port is on the roadmap once Android validates the product in the wild and we have real user signal to build against.

If you have an iPhone, the best thing you can do is tell your Android friends about CSR and check back here in a few months.
