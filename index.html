What it does

One app for the whole stop.

From the moment the lights come on to the moment you drive away — here's everything CopStopReady handles, and nothing it doesn't.

Free

Record the stop

Tap the mic and audio records straight to a PIN-locked, on-device Vault — never uploaded. The same screen shows the exact lines to say to the officer, so you're never guessing.

Free

CSR Coach

The Traffic Stop Coach walks you through the whole stop in six short steps: what to say, which documents to hand over, how to decline a search, and when to stay silent — grounded in your 5th, 4th and 1st Amendment rights. Read it once or twice at home and you’re set: in a real stop you just tap record, and your three lines are right there on the screen.

Free for now

Doc Wallet

Keep your license, registration, insurance and inspection sticker stored on-device and PIN-locked — always within reach. Renewal reminders land 90, 60, 30, 14, 7 and 3 days before anything expires, so you never drive on a lapsed doc.

Free

Rights library

The Rights Booklet and Book of Secrets go deep on your rights — what officers can and can’t do, and what holds up in court — right down to the newest laws, like New Jersey’s marijuana rules. Reading them is optional, not required: they’re here for your own knowledge, to read at home whenever you’re curious. In a real stop, CSR walks you through it.

Free

Show Docs

Forgot your physical wallet? Tap Show Docs and CopStopReady brings up your saved license, registration and insurance on one clean page — so you can still show an officer what they need. Never hand over your phone — just show them the screen. It shows only your documents; the rest of your phone stays private, the back button is locked behind your PIN, and nothing ever leaves your device. It’s a safety net for the days you leave your wallet at home — not something the app is built around.

Free for now

Fines Wallet

Every car fine with a due date, watched for you — parking & traffic tickets, tow bills, red-light & speed-camera notices, tolls. Snap a picture, add the due date, tap save. Done. Alerts turn on automatically — reminders count down 60, 30, 14, 7, 3 and 1 days before it's due, and again the day it's due — so a late fee never sneaks up and eats your wallet.

Free

Yours alone

Everything above works without an account and without a signal. No sign-up wall, no data broker, no "we value your privacy" fine print — because there's nothing to collect in the first place. That's one thing most apps can't match.
