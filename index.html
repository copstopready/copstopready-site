What it does

One app for the whole stop.

From the moment the lights come on to the day you walk into court — here's everything CopStopReady handles, and nothing it doesn't.

Free

Record the stop

Tap the mic and audio records straight to a PIN-locked, on-device Vault — never uploaded. The same screen shows the exact lines to say to the officer, so you're never guessing.

Step-by-step through a live stop: what to say, which documents to hand over, how to decline a search, and when to stay silent — grounded in your 5th, 4th and 1st Amendment rights. A Court Day Coach preps you for the hearing too.
