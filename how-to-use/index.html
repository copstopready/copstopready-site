HOW IT WORKS

CopStopReady puts your rights and a recorder in your pocket — so if you get pulled over, you know exactly what to do.

1

Pulled over? Tap the red mic — first.

It records the whole stop. That's your digital evidence, saved right here on your phone for court later. Without it, it's just his word against yours — and we all know how that ends. Always tap record first.

2

Hand over your docs, then use your lines.

When asked, hand your three documents over and follow any lawful order. When that first trick question comes, answer with your lines from the live recording screen: “I'm pleading the Fifth. I do not consent to any searches.” Then answer every question for the rest of the stop with “I want a lawyer.” Game over.

3

Read the Traffic Stop Coach — once.

Read it once or twice, whenever — it takes two minutes, tops — then never again. It's just a simple how-to guide. Once you know the game, you never touch it again.

4

It all stays on your phone.

No account. Nothing uploads. No servers, no analytics, no telemetry — a privacy-first app built for traffic stops. Nothing leaves your phone. Ever.

New to it? Run a practice stop.

A quick tap-through rehearsal, so the real thing already feels familiar — long before you ever see flashing lights.
