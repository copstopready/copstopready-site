HOW TO USE

CopStopReady in six simple steps — from the moment you're pulled over to the day you walk into court.

CopStopReady wasn't only built the safest way an app can be built — every scrap of your data stays on your device and nowhere else. It was also built to be used by anyone. Whether you're 18 with a brand-new license or 70 and have been driving for decades, these six steps take a few minutes to read and leave you CSR-certified long before you ever see flashing lights behind you. Simple on purpose — because the moment you actually need this app is the worst possible time to be learning it.

1

Pulled over? Tap the mic.

Open CopStopReady and tap the red mic the second you're stopped. It records straight to a locked, on-device vault — and it keeps recording whether the app is open or your screen is off, so your phone can sit face-down on your lap while CSR keeps listening. Every second of audio is saved right to your device, so it's there when you need it come court day. When asked, hand over the three documents the law requires — license, registration, and insurance — then let the CSR Traffic Stop Coach do the rest.

2

Let the Coach walk you through it.

Then tap the Traffic Stop Coach and follow its lead. It gives you glanceable, step-by-step prompts so you take control of the stop — when the first question comes, just answer with what's on your screen. Trick questions get shut down before they start. Be smart and use your constitutional rights; they were made for exactly this moment.

3

Stay in control. Use your rights.

The moment that first question comes, plead the 5th and stay firm — it doesn't matter if they ask two questions or fifty. One line carries you through the whole stop: “Sorry officer, I don't answer questions without a lawyer.” That's the only answer you ever need to give to their questions.

4

Do not consent.

If they ask to search your car, say it once — loud and clear: “Sorry, officer, I do not consent to any searches.” That's all it takes; you pleaded the 4th so you've put your refusal on the record. They may search anyway. If they do, don't argue and don't resist — stay calm and let it happen, because CSR is documenting every second. If that search crossed the line, you'll have the proof when it counts.

5

Keep your docs handy.

Doc Wallet stores your license, registration, and insurance on your phone as your “forgot my wallet” backup, and reminds you before any of them expire.

6

Show up ready for court.

The Court Day Coach preps you for the hearing, and a court-date warning fires on its own as the date gets close — so it never sneaks up on you.

That's the whole app.

No account, no cloud, nothing to set up — open it and go. CopStopReady is in closed testing on Google Play now, and public launch is coming.
