Privacy Policy

Effective July 21, 2026
CopStopReady is built privacy-first. We do not run servers. We do not collect user data. We do not run analytics. We do not have crash reporting. Nothing about your use of this app leaves your phone.

What stays on your phone (always)

  • Your driver license, registration, insurance, and inspection sticker photos
  • All audio recordings made during encounters
  • GPS coordinates of where each recording started and stopped (stored inside the recording metadata file, never transmitted)
  • Your PIN (encrypted in your phone hardware-backed secure storage)
  • Your scanned tickets and generated court letters
  • App settings and preferences

There is no server. There is no cloud account. There is nothing to leak, subpoena, or sell.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, address, or identifying information
  • Your contacts list
  • Analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics
  • Crash reports or error logs
  • Anything you do inside the app

Location (GPS)

Each audio recording is stamped with GPS coordinates at the moment recording starts and stops, embedded in the recording's local metadata file. This documents WHERE the encounter happened — critical evidence if your recording is ever used in court or as a complaint. The coordinates stay on your phone with the recording. We never see them. We have no servers. If you deny the location permission, recordings still save with the coordinates blank.

Reminders & notifications

Document-expiry and court-date reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They do not require a server connection, and nothing is sent to us. Renewal Reminders (all your documents, or a family plan) are an optional paid feature planned for a future update; whether free or paid, every reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device, and nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

CopStopReady is free to use today — billing is not active yet. Two paid features are planned for a future update: Ticket Fighter ($9.99 per scan) and Renewal Reminders ($1.99 per month). When they become available, both are processed by the Google Play Store, and we receive purchase confirmation only — never your payment details.

What we will never do

  • Sell your data to anyone
  • Share your data with law enforcement
  • Access your recordings, documents, or coordinates remotely
  • Send your data to advertisers
  • Add analytics or tracking in a future update without an opt-in prompt and a Privacy Policy update first

You own your data. Uninstalling CopStopReady removes everything from your device.

Future changes to this policy

If we ever change how CopStopReady handles data, for example by adding an optional feature that uses a network connection, we will (1) update this Privacy Policy before the change takes effect, (2) tell you about the change inside the app with a clear notice, and (3) make any new data-collecting feature opt-in by default. We will never enable data collection without your explicit consent, and we will never start collecting data quietly. If you do not want a new feature, you can keep using CopStopReady exactly as it works today.
